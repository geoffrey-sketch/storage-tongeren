What you can store here

The units are dry and stay at a minimum of 10 °C. Furniture, electronics, paper, clothing, textiles and collections are safe here, even in winter.

You can fill your unit right up to the ceiling, roughly 3 m high. Stack heavy items at the bottom and leave a narrow walkway free, so you can still reach what you're looking for even six months later.

What's not allowed

  • Flammable Fuel, gas canisters, paint, aerosols
  • Explosive Fireworks, ammunition, chemicals
  • Perishable Food, waste, plants
  • Living Animals, under no circumstances
  • Illegal Anything against the law
