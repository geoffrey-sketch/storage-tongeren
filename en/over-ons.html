Same building, our own name

The storage facility on Prinsenweg in Tongeren carries on as Super Storage, separate from the Genk location.

What's changing

Until now, this location was part of CityStorage. Since the split, it stands on its own under the name Super Storage. The operation, the building and the people you reach on the phone stay the same.

In practice that means: a different name on your invoice, a different email address and our own website. Your unit, your code, your rent and your access don't change. Existing customers will be notified separately about this.

Are you currently a CityStorage Tongeren customer? You don't need to do anything. If you'd still like confirmation about your file, just call +32 456 83 49 66.

The building on Prinsenweg
